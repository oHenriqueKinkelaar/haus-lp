Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a new kitchen cost in London?

It depends on size, units and worktops, but most of our kitchen projects start from around £8,000 and rise with premium materials and appliances. You'll always get a fixed written quote after your free survey — never a vague estimate.

Do you supply the kitchen or do I?

Either. We can supply and fit trade-quality units and worktops, or install a kitchen you've bought yourself. We'll advise on the best value route during your consultation.

How long does a kitchen installation take?

Most kitchens are fitted in 5–10 working days once materials have arrived. We agree a clear timeline with you before starting.

Are you insured and certified?

Yes. We're CBWA-certified kitchen fitters with Enhanced DBS-checked operatives and £5M public liability insurance on every job.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Greater London plus Surrey, Essex, Kent and Berkshire.

Do you handle plumbing and electrics too?

Yes — our team covers the full fit including first-fix plumbing and electrical work, tiling and finishing, so you deal with one company throughout.
